# Brand Logo Contract

| Context | Light theme | Dark theme | Accessible behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header | `only-logo.png` | `only-logo.png` | Link named `Ir al inicio de OneITB`; image decorative |
| Full brand surfaces | `logo-oneitb.png` | `logo-oneitb-dark-mode.png` | One image named by `BrandLockup`, or fully decorative |

The contract forbids image filters, glow, DOM-composed wordmarks and simultaneous
light/dark full images in the rendered component tree.
