## Manual smoke

1. Start the frontend and open `http://localhost:5173/` while logged out.
2. Verify the experimental comparison laboratory is absent.
3. Verify header symbol and all landing calls to action at 320 px, tablet and desktop widths.
4. Toggle Clean Tech / Tech Noir after login or preload `oneitb-theme`; confirm softened surfaces and readable contrast.
5. Move a fine pointer across the page; confirm the spotlight is subtle and never blocks controls.
6. Enable reduced motion in the operating system/browser; reload and confirm all content is immediately visible.
7. Navigate away and back; confirm no duplicate observer behavior or console error.
